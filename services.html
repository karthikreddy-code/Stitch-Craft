Need to Know?

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our embroidery services and custom orders.

Can I request a custom embroidery design?

Yes. Share your design idea, reference image, or personalization details with us to discuss a custom creation.

Can you embroider a company logo on uniforms?

We can discuss logo embroidery for uniforms and workwear. Send your logo and order quantity to enquire about the requirements.

Do you accept bulk orders?

You can enquire about bulk embroidery. Include the quantity, product type, and design so we can discuss your order.

How do I get pricing and delivery details?

Contact us with your design and order details. We can then discuss pricing and the estimated completion or delivery time.

Can I choose thread colors and embroidery placement?

You can share your preferred thread colors, size, and placement when enquiring about a custom design.
