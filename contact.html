Frequently Asked Questions

Here are some common questions about our embroidery services.

Can I request custom embroidery?

Yes. Share your design idea, text, or reference image with us through the enquiry form to discuss a custom order.

Can you embroider a company logo?

We can discuss logo embroidery and digitizing. Please provide your logo file and the item you want embroidered.
